Rules

  • No AI, of any kind. No Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, or similar — for code, layout, or debugging.
  • Fair game: official Workday docs, this project's own existing .pmd files, your own memory and notes.
  • Solo, one file. A single .pmd page, built and previewed in Studio.
  • Live endpoint this week (exception): wire your grid to any real, live endpoint you already have access to in your own tenant/app — Staffing API, an existing custom BO, whatever you're set up for. The exact data doesn't have to match anyone else's; what's judged is that the endpoint and grid are wired correctly.
  • Don't wait on new provisioning. Use an endpoint that already exists and works today — this round isn't the time to stand up a new business object.
  • Submit by showing your Preview to the judge when the clock runs out, finished or not.
