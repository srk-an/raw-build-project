Upload your app export

  1. In Studio, export the latest build of your app. You get a .zip named like resolveiq_zyfpsx.zip.
  2. Drop that zip below without unzipping it.
  3. Review the findings, then download the Excel sheet and fill in the Partner Comment column.

Get the sheet

The Excel file has four tabs: the build sheet (Build Checklist + Review Feedback, same columns as Workday's), Comments, Summary (live Action/Advice counters plus a pass/fail line for every rule) and Details (every individual line behind the grouped rows). For Google Sheets, click Copy for Google Sheets, create a blank sheet and paste into cell A1.
