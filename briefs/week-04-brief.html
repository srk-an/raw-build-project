Rules

  • No AI, of any kind. No Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, or similar — for code, layout, or debugging.
  • Fair game: official Workday docs, this project's own existing .pmd files, your own memory and notes.
  • Solo, two files. An edit page and a view page, wired together by a flow in the AMD.
  • No live business object or endpoint. Everything is carried via flowVariables between the two pages — nobody's blocked waiting on data or Studio provisioning.
  • Submit OK on the edit page before time's up, so the judge can see the view page actually display what you typed.
